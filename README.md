### Hi, I'm Adam 👋
 
I build niche tools that are too tedious, too pricey or too fiddly to do by hand – usually because I wanted one myself and it didn't exist.
 
By day I work with integrations and logistics software. Before that: industrial engineering at Chalmers, economics at Lund, SEO, BI dashboards, and six countries' worth of moving boxes. I like the spot where business problems turn into working software: data flows, integrations, scoring models and interfaces people actually enjoy using.
